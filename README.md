Fusion 360 has no built-in way to make the viewport show your model at actual physical size — zoom level is always relative, so a 10cm part might render as 3cm or 15cm on screen depending on your last zoom action. That makes it hard to eyeball real-world fit, ergonomics, or proportions without exporting, printing, or physically measuring your screen.
Skala fixes that with one click. It reads your display's actual DPI and instantly sets the viewport scale so that 1cm in the model is exactly 1cm on your monitor. Useful any time you want a quick sense of scale.


 It's a lightweight, dependency-free add-in that lives in the viewport's navigation toolbar, and works on both Windows and Mac. Just use the banana for scale.
